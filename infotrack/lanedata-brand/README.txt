TYPOGRAPHY
----------

  Wordmark:  Bricolage Grotesque, weight 800, letter-spacing -4%
  Free on Google Fonts: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Bricolage+Grotesque

  The SVG lockups reference this font by name. If the renderer
  doesn't have it installed, it falls back to a system sans.
  To make the SVG fully self-contained, open it in your design
  tool (Figma, Illustrator) and convert the wordmark text to
  outlines/paths.


HOW TO USE ON INSTAGRAM
-----------------------

  Profile photo:   Upload avatar-1080.png. IG crops to circle automatically.
  Posts:          Use mark-square.svg as a corner watermark, or build
                   templates with lockup-stacked.svg as the header.
  Stories:         Place mark-square.svg as a sticker (export as PNG
                   from your design tool first if needed).


RULES
-----

  ✓  Use the mark as drawn.
  ✗  Don't stretch, skew, rotate, or re-color it.
  ✗  Don't add effects (drop shadow, gradient, outline).
  ✗  Don't place it on a busy photo without a solid backplate.

  Minimum size: 20px tall. Below that the eyes lose their proportion.
  Clear space: leave at least one eye-width of empty space on every side.
